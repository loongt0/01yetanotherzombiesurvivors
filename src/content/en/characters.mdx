Yet Another Zombie Survivors characters in version 1.0 are SWAT, Tank, Engineer, Huntress, Ghost, Medic, Pyro, Mechanic, and Ranger. A run begins with one Survivor and can expand through SOS recruitment to a maximum squad of three. Ranger is the developer-confirmed ninth character and unlocks at Survival Level 175, while several older unlock conditions remain unconfirmed.

## How Squad Recruitment and Leader Choice Work

The [official game website](https://yazs.awesomegamesstudio.com/) explains that a run starts with one Survivor. SOS calls can recruit up to two more characters, creating solo, duo, or three-person team options. This means a character's practical value depends partly on who joins afterward. A starting leader, current weapon plan, and available synergy can all change the same character's role.

Tank is described as durable and has a researched leader benefit involving squad armor. Huntress is associated with ranged critical-hit pressure and boss damage, while Ghost is described as melee-focused. Ranger's documented themes include a crossbow, animal companions, critical or Luck support, and healing tools. Full leader bonuses for the remaining characters and exact numerical effects are unconfirmed.

The researched [Tank character reference](https://yetanotherzombie.wiki.gg/wiki/Tank) lists Shotgun, Pump-Action Shotgun, Minigun, and Rocket Launcher. Minigun uses the Kinetic damage tag, while Rocket Launcher uses the Explosive tag. The same material connects both advanced weapons with Iron Will and Tank's Rank 2 progression. Current in-game wording should be checked before treating every historical unlock detail as unchanged.
