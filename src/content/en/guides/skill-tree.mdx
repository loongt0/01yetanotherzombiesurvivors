The yet another zombie survivors skill tree now has Rank 5 progression for each of the game's nine Survivors. Version 1.0 adds five nodes per Survivor at that rank: two synergy nodes, two passive-improvement nodes, and one team-wide passive that works while the Survivor is present. Build the characters you actually field first, because team composition determines which synergy nodes can contribute.

## Nine Survivors Mean Nine Character Trees

Awesome Games Studio's official version 1.0 launch material describes progression through perks, abilities, and nodes across nine skill trees. The confirmed roster is SWAT, Tank, Engineer, Huntress, Ghost, Medic, Pyro, Mechanic, and Ranger. Ranger is the ninth Survivor added in the full release.

These character trees are not the same thing as Survival Level. The official release states that Ranger unlocks at Survival Level 175, but it does not call 175 the maximum level. A character's own level, account progression, skill-tree Rank, and unlock milestones must be described separately.

The collected sources do not provide a complete official export of every node name and value. When a page discusses a specific node, it should use the current in-game wording or mark the detail **unconfirmed**. An older wiki screenshot can help locate a topic, but it cannot override version 1.0 patch notes.

## What Rank 5 Adds

The [official full-release notes](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) state that each Survivor receives two new synergy nodes, two passive-improvement nodes, and one new passive node for the whole team. That is five additions per Survivor tied to the expanded rank. The whole-team passive applies when the relevant Survivor is present, so it belongs in squad planning rather than a purely solo stat list.

Rank 5 also expands cross-Survivor interactions. A team can have an attractive combination on paper yet fail to produce the expected effect if the required node is still locked. Before copying a build, check the exact Survivor Rank and inspect the node that names the teammate or ability.

The sources do not establish one universal order for purchasing all Rank 5 nodes. A player focused on one squad can reasonably value its active synergy nodes, while another player may value a passive that helps several teams. Exact costs, reset behavior, and a globally optimal purchase order are **unconfirmed** in the collected evidence.

## How Synergy Nodes Work in Team Planning

The current [Steam synergy guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3352985777) describes synergy upgrades as passive ability improvements unlocked through tiers II, III, IV, and V. It separates simple synergies, which improve one character's ability, from mutual synergies, which improve both sides. The guide maps these links across all nine Survivors.

Its version 1.0 examples include Engineer and Ranger with Huntress, Ghost, or Mechanic; Ghost and Mechanic with Engineer or Ranger; Huntress and Medic with Pyro or Ranger; and Tank and Pyro with SWAT or Medic. Those trios contain five recorded synergy links. They are useful planning evidence, but synergy count alone does not prove the highest damage, safest run, or fastest boss clear.

The right reading order is node first, team second, result third. Confirm the node is unlocked, select the associated ability during the run, and then test the effect under known conditions. If any of those steps is missing, the build result cannot be attributed confidently to the synergy.

## General Progression Versus Survivor Progression

The existing research distinguishes account-wide General upgrades from Survivor-specific weapon, ability, badge, and synergy progression. The official sources confirm the new character-tree additions but do not publish a complete recommended split between General points and character points. Avoid inventing a fixed percentage or a universal first purchase.

A practical evidence-based approach is to support the current team instead of distributing resources evenly without a goal. If Ghost is the leader in most runs, Ghost's weapon and relevant team nodes will affect more tests than an unused character's synergy. If Huntress is the anchor, her weapon, Arrow Rain, status, and teammate nodes should be inspected together.

For build-specific applications, use the [Ghost build guide](/characters/ghost/build/) and [Huntress build guide](/characters/huntress/build/). The [build hub](/builds/) explains how leader choice, damage plan, items, and teammates connect to permanent tree progress.

## What Is the Current Character Level Cap?

A current Steam discussion asks how far a Survivor must level to fill the remaining upgrades. Two players independently answer 224, and one states that Tank has reached level 224 while SWAT is at 214. This is relevant current version 1.0 evidence, but the thread contains no developer confirmation.

The official release notes confirm Rank 5 but do not publish a Survivor level cap. Therefore, the accurate wording is “level 224, community-confirmed in version 1.0,” not “official cap 224.” A later patch could change the amount of progression required, so the live tree should be checked after updates.

The [max level and Rank 5 guide](/guides/max-level-and-rank-5/) keeps this distinction explicit. It also separates Survivor level 224 from Ranger's Survival Level 175 unlock and the Team Bond cap of 250. These values describe different systems and are not interchangeable.

## Skill Tree Priorities for a New Build

First, choose the leader and the two likely recruits. Second, identify the named synergy nodes that connect those characters. Third, check whether the build is weapon-focused, ability-focused, status-focused, defensive, or mixed for a documented reason.

Prioritize nodes that the selected run can actually activate. A passive improvement to an unused ability cannot carry a build merely because it is at Rank 5. A team-wide passive may have broader value, but the official notes only confirm its existence; its exact value depends on the specific Survivor and current tooltip.

Do not claim a perfect point order where the source material has no controlled comparison. Mark exact costs, numerical return per point, and final optimization **unconfirmed** until verified in game. The guide's purpose is to show how to make a traceable decision, not to manufacture a spreadsheet from missing data.

## Missions and Team Bond Support Progression

Version 1.0 missions originally awarded Friendship, a permanent boost to Cash and Specialization Point gains. Hotfix 1.0.0c renamed the system Team Bond to clarify that the bonus is shared by the whole team. More Cash and Specialization Points can speed permanent progression, but Team Bond is not itself a skill-tree Rank.

The official maximum Team Bond value is 250. This does not mean a Survivor reaches level 250, and it does not change Ranger's level-175 unlock requirement. Read the [Friendship and Team Bond guide](/guides/friendship-and-team-bond/) for the exact rename, reward loop, display location, and cap.

The reliable skill-tree baseline is nine trees, official Rank 5 additions, and community-mapped synergy links. Full node tables, exact costs, and one optimal account-wide order remain **unconfirmed**. Always compare current in-game text with any guide written before the latest hotfix.
