This Yet Another Zombie Survivors achievement guide starts with the live platform list: Steam currently shows 229 achievements, while the full-release announcement says version 1.0 added 88, including 64 Story achievements. Treat those as different figures rather than adding them together. Use the live list for exact objective text and use version-matched evidence before following a route.

## Check the Current List Before Planning

The [Steam global achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2163330/achievements/) is the primary reference for names, descriptions, and the current total. Its completion percentages change as more people play, so a percentage captured today is not a permanent difficulty rating. Search the live page for the objective you want before committing a full run.

An objective description confirms what must be done, but it may not explain the easiest route. When Steam names only a map or final boss, do not add a character, difficulty, or time restriction without another source. Any extra condition absent from current evidence is **unconfirmed**.

## Separate the 1.0 Additions

The developer's [version 1.0 release notes](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) state that the full release added 88 achievements. The same announcement identifies 64 as Story achievements connected to the expanded narrative progression. This is an addition count, not the total platform count.

Version 1.0 also introduced the Survivors' Camp, main and Survivor missions, Ranger, Bio Lab, Boss Rush, Friendship progression, and expanded skill trees. Those systems explain why an Early Access checklist cannot cover the full current game. Exact requirements still need the live achievement text or a current in-game record.

## Keep Torments Out of the Achievement Total

The official release notes list 28 Torments, but explicitly describe them as separate from the achievement system. They are optional challenges and are not required for 100 percent platform achievement completion. Do not add 28 to the Steam total or report a missing Torment as a missing achievement.

Torments can still be useful late-game objectives, especially after normal progression is complete. Their challenge conditions may demand specialized builds, but that does not make them platform achievements. Individual Torment rewards and current balance are **unconfirmed** unless a version-matched source documents them.

## Group Achievements by the Required Action

A practical checklist separates natural progression from deliberate challenge runs. Story progress, Survivor ranks, map clears, mode unlocks, and account-level milestones can advance while learning the game. Restriction runs, hidden interactions, exact damage tasks, and specific bosses deserve separate attempts once the relevant systems are available.

This grouping prevents a player from spending a long run on an objective that belongs in a menu or different mode. Record the map, mode, difficulty, squad, and restriction before each targeted attempt. Return to the achievement panel afterward and verify that the objective actually registered.

## Use Official Unlock Conditions Where Available

The 1.0 announcement confirms that Ranger unlocks at Survival Level 175. It also confirms that Bio Lab opens after completing Dead Terminal on Default, while Boss Rush unlocks after completing Hardcore for the applicable map. These are stronger references than an older guide written before those features existed.

Do not generalize one confirmed unlock into a complete achievement route. A map being available does not prove which difficulty an unrelated objective needs, and a Survivor unlock does not establish the best build for that Survivor. Requirements not displayed by Steam, the game, or the developer remain **unconfirmed**.

## Handle I'm The Boss by Its Exact Name

Players sometimes search for “I'm Boss Here,” but the live Steam entry is **I'm The Boss**. Its displayed condition is to defeat the final boss of the Isolated City. [PlayStationTrophies](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/trophy/i-m-the-boss.html) and [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/achievement/i-m-the-boss.html) show the same name and condition.

That cross-platform agreement confirms the map and target. It does not state a required Survivor, build, difficulty, or completion time. Defeating a regular boss or the final boss of a different arena does not match the wording.

## Treat Sanji as a Versioned Secret

The current Curly Little Potato objective is to find Sanji the Rabbit. The maintained [wiki achievement entry](https://yetanotherzombie.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements_%28Survivors%29) and later player reports place the route on Vile Wasteland, using one Potato and a house window near the east or northeast boundary. The official 1.0 notes confirm that the reward changed from Jade Amulet to Easter Egg.

Older videos show players collecting three Potatoes before the trigger. That footage documents an earlier mechanic rather than a second current route. Potato drop rates and the claim that Power Overwhelming must be completed first remain **unconfirmed**.

## Use Older Guides as Historical Leads

The widely used [Steam community completion guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3006467623) contains many routes, but its own version coverage stops at 0.9. It can help identify an old objective, item, or interaction, but it cannot verify the 88 achievements added in 1.0. Recheck every copied instruction against the live list.

A 2023 guide may also preserve an achievement that changed or received a new reward later. Keep its date and version attached to the note instead of silently presenting it as current. If no current reproduction exists, mark the route **unconfirmed** rather than inventing missing steps.

## Build a Reliable Completion Record

For each unfinished achievement, save the exact title, displayed condition, platform, game version, and date checked. Add the relevant map or menu, then link the official source and one independent route where possible. This creates a checklist that survives patches better than a copied table.

After an attempt, record success or failure and any conditions that were actually visible. One successful run proves that a route can work, not that every part of the build was mandatory. Separate observed facts from player recommendations so later updates can be audited cleanly.
