## When to Choose Minigun

Choose Minigun when you expect Tank to fight at close range and can maintain that position. According to the official range-system explanation, Minigun damage gradually decreases as distance grows. That makes spacing part of the build decision rather than a cosmetic preference.

The developer does not provide an exact distance threshold or falloff percentage in the published notes. It is therefore unsafe to claim a precise “best range,” damage loss, or breakpoint. Use the live weapon panel and a controlled run to determine how close Tank must remain in the current build.

Minigun may be the better practical choice when the team plan already keeps Tank near targets. That conclusion follows from the official falloff behavior, not from an unsupported claim about attack speed, damage type, or boss DPS. If the run regularly forces Tank away from enemies, the official notes give a clear reason to reconsider it.

## When to Choose Rocket Launcher

Choose Rocket Launcher when positioning is uncertain or when the team needs a weapon that remains viable across different ranges. The same official update calls Rocket Launcher a fully viable option at any range and describes it as a third, all-around choice for team composition. This is the strongest official reason to select it.

“All-around” does not mean officially best at every task. The developer does not state that Rocket Launcher always clears crowds faster, wins every boss fight, or outdamages Minigun. It means the published range notes do not impose the same increasing-distance penalty described for Minigun.

This flexibility matters when a run changes pace or forces frequent movement. It also reduces the need to build the comparison around one fixed engagement distance. Exact projectile behavior, blast coverage, and target limits are not specified in the official sources used here.
