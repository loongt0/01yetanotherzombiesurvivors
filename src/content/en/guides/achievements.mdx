## I'm The Boss Achievement — Searched as “I'm Boss Here”

The current [official Steam achievement list](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2163330/achievements/) names the objective **I'm The Boss** and describes it as “Defeat the final boss of the Isolated City.” Search queries often shorten or misremember the name as “I'm Boss Here,” but both intents belong on this page rather than on a separate thin article.

Based on the official wording, the target is the final boss tied specifically to the Isolated City. Defeating an ordinary boss or a final boss on another map does not match that displayed condition. The Steam description does not specify a required Survivor, difficulty, build, or time limit, so those extra restrictions remain unconfirmed unless the live game presents them during the attempt.

The same name and condition appear independently on [PlayStationTrophies](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/trophy/i-m-the-boss.html) and [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/achievement/i-m-the-boss.html). That cross-platform match supports the map and boss target, but it does not add requirements absent from the displayed description.
