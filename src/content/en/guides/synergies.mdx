Yet Another Zombie Survivors synergies are cross-character upgrade interactions that become available when compatible Survivors and their relevant abilities appear in the same squad. Documented examples include Huntress's Bear Trap creating Tank's Sawblade Drone, Tank's Fury Unleashed gaining Medic-related health regeneration, and Engineer's Electrocution triggering Huntress's Frost Arrow. Version 1.0 expands these systems through Rank 5 and additional character-specific synergy nodes.

## What Counts as a Survivor Synergy

The [official game website](https://yazs.awesomegamesstudio.com/) identifies team synergies as a core part of building a run. A synergy is more specific than simply placing two powerful characters beside each other. It links an ability, weapon interaction, passive effect, or upgrade choice to another Survivor's presence. The actual trigger and wording must be checked in the current in-game skill tree.

Some combinations improve one character's ability without providing an equally strong effect in the opposite direction. Others connect both Survivors through mutually useful upgrades or shared build goals. The researched community guide distinguishes these patterns rather than assuming every pairing is symmetrical. Exact current damage scaling and every trigger condition are unconfirmed unless independently verified.
