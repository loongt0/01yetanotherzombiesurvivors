This Yet Another Zombie Survivors achievements guide organizes the current list into progression, Story, map, mode, restriction, boss, and hidden-objective work. Steam currently displays 229 achievements, and the official 1.0 announcement says 88 were new at full release. Start with objectives that advance normal progression, then isolate special conditions into deliberate runs.

## Use Steam as the Live Checklist

The [Steam achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2163330/achievements/) supplies the current titles, objective descriptions, total, and changing completion rates. It is the strongest source for deciding whether an older guide still uses the correct name. Check it again after major patches because a static article cannot guarantee that every entry remains unchanged.

Completion rate is not the same as objective difficulty. New achievements can show low percentages simply because fewer players have reached the new content. Any permanent difficulty ranking inferred only from a live percentage is **unconfirmed**.

## Understand What Version 1.0 Added

The [developer release announcement](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) records 88 new achievements for version 1.0. It says 64 belong to Story and describes a larger progression structure built around the Camp, missions, and individual Survivors. These additions explain why an Early Access list is incomplete.

The announcement also lists 15 main-story missions, 45 Survivor missions, and 19 repeatable missions. Those mission counts describe content, not automatically one achievement per mission. Do not convert a mission total into an achievement total unless the live list makes that relationship explicit.

## Plan Natural Progression First

Begin with objectives that occur while learning maps, recruiting a squad, collecting resources, and raising permanent progression. This phase reveals which Survivors, modes, and systems are available without forcing a specialized restriction. It also gives later attempts a better-developed account.

Record which achievements unlock naturally rather than relying on memory. A natural unlock can expose a weapon, item, or mode needed for another objective, but the exact reward must be checked in the current client. Reward links copied only from an old guide are **unconfirmed**.

## Separate Story and Survivor Work

Story achievements belong to the full-release narrative systems and should be tracked through the current Camp and mission interface. Survivor-specific work may require playing with a particular character or advancing an individual tree. Older guides cannot document Ranger's full path because Ranger arrived in 1.0.

The official announcement confirms Ranger as the ninth Survivor and places the unlock at Survival Level 175. That is a verified gate, but it does not state that every Ranger achievement unlocks automatically afterward. Individual Ranger objectives need their own live wording.

## Track Maps and Modes Independently

Bio Lab unlocks after Dead Terminal is completed on Default, according to the official 1.0 notes. Boss Rush becomes available for a map after completing its Hardcore mode, and the final Boss Rush boss appears around the ten-minute point. These facts help diagnose why a related objective is not yet available.

They do not prove the requirements for every map achievement. Before a targeted run, note the exact arena, mode, difficulty, and objective text. Any assumed shortcut or alternate mode not supported by the achievement wording remains **unconfirmed**.

## Do Not Count Torments as Achievements

Version 1.0 includes 28 Torments, but the developer explicitly separates them from the achievement system and says they are not needed for 100 percent achievements. They can be treated as optional endgame challenges after platform completion. Their presence should never inflate the Steam total.

Some third-party pages place Torments beside endgame achievements because both are difficult. That is a useful navigation choice, not proof that they share completion tracking. Keep the two checklists separate in the article and in any spreadsheet used for progress.

## Handle Exact Boss Objectives Literally

The live list names **I'm The Boss** and tells the player to defeat the final boss of the Isolated City. The same condition appears on [PlayStationTrophies](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/trophy/i-m-the-boss.html) and [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/achievement/i-m-the-boss.html). Three matching platform references support the target without adding hidden rules.

Do not substitute a normal boss or a final boss from another map. No cited description requires a specific Survivor, build, or time limit. Those extra conditions are **unconfirmed** until the live game displays them.

## Isolate Hidden and Item Objectives

Curly Little Potato asks the player to find Sanji the Rabbit. Current community sources place Sanji at a house window on the east-to-northeast edge of Vile Wasteland after obtaining one Potato. The 1.0 release notes independently confirm that its reward is now Easter Egg rather than Jade Amulet.

Older videos record a three-Potato version of the secret, so version labeling is essential. The exact Potato drop rate and a claimed Power Overwhelming prerequisite remain **unconfirmed**. Keep those unknowns visible instead of turning them into mandatory steps.

## Audit Early Access Guides

The [Steam community 100 percent guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3006467623) is useful for historical objectives, but its comprehensive coverage ends at version 0.9. A 2023 article may list only 40 achievements because that was the available scope at the time. Neither source can replace the current Steam list.

Use old instructions to form a test, then reproduce the route on the current version. Save the platform notification and compare the displayed reward. If the result cannot be reproduced, label the old route obsolete or **unconfirmed** rather than silently repairing it with guesses.

## Finish With a Focused Cleanup Pass

After normal progression, filter the remaining list by map, mode, Survivor, restriction, and menu action. Combine objectives only when their displayed requirements are compatible. This reduces repeated long runs without claiming a universal fastest roadmap.

Verify every completed item in the active platform list. Keep Steam totals separate from PlayStation trophies and Xbox Gamerscore because platform structures differ. A complete record should include the date and version so the next patch can be checked efficiently.
