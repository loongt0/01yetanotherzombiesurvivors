The current yet another zombie survivors max level for one Survivor is reported as 224 by two version 1.0 players, including one who reached level 224 with Tank. The developer's official notes do not publish that number, so it is community-confirmed rather than official. Rank 5, Survival Level 175, and Team Bond 250 describe different systems and are not alternative answers to the same question.

## Survivor Max Level: 224 Is Community-Confirmed

A current [Steam level-cap discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2163330/discussions/0/580554442217125249/) asks how far a Survivor must progress to finish remaining upgrades. Two separate players answer 224. One states that Tank has reached 224 and SWAT is at 214.

This is direct version 1.0 player evidence, but no developer joins the thread. The official full-release announcement confirms expanded progression without publishing the final character-level number. The accurate label is therefore “Survivor level cap 224, confirmed by current community reports.”

A future update could add nodes or change the required level. Check the current character screen after patches, especially if the tree still has incomplete upgrades at 224 or continues granting meaningful levels. Any newer value without matching evidence is **unconfirmed**.

## Rank 5 Is a Skill-Tree Tier

The [official version 1.0 release notes](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) add Rank 5 to every Survivor. Rank 5 expands each tree with two synergy nodes, two passive-improvement nodes, and one passive that benefits the whole team while that Survivor is present. This is official and does not depend on the community level-cap thread.

Rank 5 does not mean that a Survivor's maximum numerical level is five. It is the fifth tier of character progression. Reaching or filling that tier can require many character levels, which is why players in the cap discussion refer to remaining upgrades well above level 100.

The collected sources do not publish the exact level requirement for every individual Rank 5 node. Use the [skill tree guide](/guides/skill-tree/) to plan around the documented node types and current squad synergies. Exact point costs and a universal unlock order remain **unconfirmed**.

## Survival Level 175 Unlocks Ranger

The official release notes state that Ranger unlocks at Survival Level 175. This is an account or survival progression milestone tied to a character unlock, not an official statement that Survival Level ends at 175. Players can reach the Ranger requirement while still having incomplete individual Survivor trees.

The distinction matters because search snippets can show “level 175” next to Ranger and make it look like a maximum. The source wording is an unlock requirement. Do not replace the community-reported Survivor cap of 224 with 175, and do not claim a Survival Level cap where none is provided.

The current official launch post also describes nine skill trees after Ranger joins the roster. It does not state a maximum account level. Any claimed Survival Level cap remains **unconfirmed** in the collected evidence.

## Team Bond 250 Is Not a Character Level

The [official Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2163330/announcements/) record that hotfix 1.0.0c confirms a maximum Team Bond value of 250. Team Bond is the new name for Friendship, the shared permanent bonus to Cash and Specialization Point gains. It does not represent Survivor experience or the number of skill-tree levels.

The patch fixes an incorrect maximum-total calculation and adds a clearer tooltip to the second run end screen. This makes 250 an official current system cap, but only for Team Bond. Read the [Friendship and Team Bond guide](/guides/friendship-and-team-bond/) for the full mission and resource loop.

Do not describe 250 as a percentage without a verified in-game formula. The source establishes the maximum value and the resources affected, not the exact return for every point. Per-point scaling is **unconfirmed**.

The collected evidence does not support converting one number into another.

## How Max Level Affects Builds

A completed Survivor tree can access more permanent improvements and synergy options than a partially progressed tree. Version 1.0 makes that gap more important by adding Rank 5 nodes, including team-wide passives. Two players testing the same squad may therefore obtain different results if their trees are at different stages.

When sharing a build, record the leader's level, relevant Rank 5 nodes, teammates, arena, mode, and current patch. This does not require inventing a full numerical damage model. It simply prevents a maxed character's result from being presented as guaranteed for a new account.

The [build hub](/builds/) uses this rule when comparing team concepts. The [Ghost build](/characters/ghost/build/) and [Huntress build](/characters/huntress/build/) also separate official 1.0 changes from older experiments. Exact damage gained from every level remains **unconfirmed**.

## What to Do When a Tree Looks Incomplete

First, verify that the game is on version 1.0 or later and that the intended Survivor is receiving progression. Second, inspect whether the missing upgrade belongs to Rank 5 or depends on another condition shown in the current interface. Third, compare the live result with the community-reported cap of 224 rather than assuming a lower historical cap still applies.

If a Survivor reaches 224 and the tree still appears incomplete, record the current patch and capture the node requirement. The available official notes do not provide a complete node-by-node cap table, so the discrepancy should be marked **unconfirmed** and checked with current developer support or patch notes. Do not fabricate a hidden prestige requirement.

The reliable answer remains narrow: level 224 is the best current community evidence for an individual Survivor, Rank 5 is official, Ranger unlocks at Survival Level 175, and Team Bond caps at 250. Those facts answer separate questions and should remain separated on every progression page.
