Yet another zombie survivors friendship is the old name for Team Bond. Yet another zombie survivors Team Bond is a permanent, shared bonus to the Cash and Specialization Points earned by the whole team after completing missions. Hotfix 1.0.0c changed the name, added a clearer end-screen tooltip, and confirmed a maximum Team Bond value of 250.

## Friendship Was Renamed Team Bond

Version 1.0 launched the system under the name Friendship. The [official Steam announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2163330/announcements/) record the 1.0.0c hotfix that renamed it Team Bond because the value is shared across the team rather than belonging to one pair of characters. Search results, videos, and launch-day discussions may still use the old term.

Both names refer to the same progression mechanic in the collected official material. A page should not present Friendship and Team Bond as two separate currencies. It should answer the old search term immediately, then use the current Team Bond name for present-version instructions.

The rename also corrects a common misunderstanding. This is not a romance system, a dialogue-only affinity score, or a per-character relationship meter in the available evidence. It is a shared permanent resource-gain bonus.

## What Team Bond Actually Increases

The [official full-release announcement](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) states that completing missions rewards Friendship and permanently increases Cash and Specialization Points received after each run. A developer answer on Steam independently confirms the same two resource gains. The developer also says the bonus is calculated on the run end screens.

The sources do not state that Team Bond directly increases weapon damage, health, critical chance, or ability cooldown. Do not add combat statistics that are not present in the official description. Its value is indirect: greater resource gains support permanent meta-progression over time.

The collected evidence also does not provide a complete conversion table from each Team Bond point to a percentage bonus. If the live tooltip shows a current formula, it should be recorded from the game before publication. Until then, the exact per-point scaling is **unconfirmed**.

## How to Earn Team Bond

The official release notes connect the reward to completed missions. Version 1.0 adds 15 main-story missions, 45 Survivor missions, and 19 repeatable missions. The collected sources do not provide a verified point value for every individual mission, so no invented farming table should be published.

Missions appear through the Survivors' Camp and Briefing Room systems introduced with version 1.0. The Mission Log records storyline progress, shows requirements that may block a narrative thread, and allows an incomplete mission to be retried manually. Hotfix 1.0.0c also changes new missions so they appear in the Mission Log after their briefing without requiring a run first.

The direct progression loop is therefore simple: read or select a mission, complete its objective, receive Team Bond, and observe the resource bonus on a later run-end calculation. If a mission does not award the expected amount, verify the current patch and completion state. Exact fastest farming routes remain **unconfirmed**.

## Where to See the Bonus

Developer Kalafiorek explains in a Steam answer that the bonus appears in the run-end calculations. Players in that discussion asked for a tooltip because the launch version did not explain the stat clearly. Hotfix 1.0.0c then adds a Team Bond explanation to the second end screen.

This sequence provides two-source confirmation for the display location. Use the second end screen after a run and inspect the Cash and Specialization Point calculation. The research does not establish that every interface elsewhere in the game shows the same breakdown.

If the tooltip is missing, check that the game is updated beyond the initial 1.0 release. Platform patch timing can differ, but the collected sources do not provide a verified delay for each console. Any platform-specific discrepancy is **unconfirmed** until checked on that platform.

## Team Bond Maximum Is 250

The 1.0.0c hotfix fixes an incorrect maximum-total calculation and explicitly states that the maximum Team Bond value is 250. This is an official cap for the Team Bond mechanic. It is not the maximum Survivor level, Survival Level, or skill-tree Rank.

The same fix mattered to the “Survivor Crossing” Torment, which had become unobtainable when the maximum total was wrong. That connection explains why the cap appears in a bug-fix entry rather than the original launch overview. No further official source in the collected material changes the value after 1.0.0c.

Do not interpret 250 as 250 percent unless the current game explicitly displays that conversion. The patch note confirms the maximum value, not an independently verified percentage formula. Per-point bonus scaling remains **unconfirmed**.

## Team Bond and Skill-Tree Progression

Team Bond increases the resources used in broader meta-progression, while Survivor skill trees define character upgrades and synergies. Version 1.0 adds Rank 5 to every character tree, including new synergy nodes, passive improvements, and a whole-team passive. These systems support one another but are not the same progression track.

Keeping these guides linked prevents the number 250 from being mistaken for a character level.
