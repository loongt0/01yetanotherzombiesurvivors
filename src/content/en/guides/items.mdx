## How Item Chests Work During a Run

The [official community item index](https://yetanotherzombie.wiki.gg/wiki/Items_%28Survivors%29) describes items as passive effects that last for the remainder of the current run. Item chests appear on the arena map, and a gold arrow indicates the relevant direction. Opening the chest presents four choices according to the researched wiki entry. This makes each item selection a meaningful build decision rather than a permanent account upgrade.

Chest timing, spawn frequency, and whether every older entry remains available in version 1.0 are unconfirmed. The collected sources do not support inventing a fixed number of chests per run or a guaranteed item sequence. Arena conditions, movement, and the current team can influence whether collecting a chest is practical. Treat exact appearance rules as unverified unless the live game or developer documentation confirms them.

The [official game website](https://yazs.awesomegamesstudio.com/) distinguishes weapons, abilities, items, synergies, and persistent progression. This distinction prevents a temporary chest bonus from being mistaken for a permanent General upgrade. The current wording of each item's duration should still be checked on its live tooltip.

Those systems may change which temporary item choices feel valuable, but they do not justify inventing an item formula.
