Yet Another Zombie Survivors weapon attack speed vs weapon cooldown compares two different statistics rather than two names for the same effect. Weapon Attack Speed affects the rhythm of weapon attacks, while Weapon Cooldown Reduction affects the interval or recovery before another attack cycle. The community stat index, historical renamed labels, Modchip, and Sugar Rush support that distinction, while exact current formulas and caps are unconfirmed.

The [community wiki statistics reference](https://yetanotherzombie.wiki.gg/wiki/Stats_%28Survivors%29) places Weapon Attack Speed and Weapon Cooldown Reduction among separate weapon attributes. This source directly supports treating them as different values rather than interchangeable wording. The distinction matters because a weapon can spend time attacking and also spend time waiting before another cycle. The collected material does not provide a complete verified version 1.0 formula for either statistic.

A player comparing upgrades should therefore inspect which named attribute the current item or skill actually changes. A tooltip mentioning attack speed should not be silently rewritten as cooldown reduction. Similarly, a cooldown bonus should not be assumed to increase every individual projectile's firing frequency. Weapon Attack Speed refers to how quickly the weapon performs attacks during its active firing or attack phase. It is the modern label associated with the older Weapon Fire Rate terminology. This can matter when a weapon repeatedly attacks while its cycle is active. The collected evidence does not provide a universal numerical multiplier for every weapon.

Different weapons may present their attacks through individual shots, groups, or sustained sequences. A grouped projectile attack can also interact with the full-release critical-hit changes described by the developer. Therefore, faster attacks do not automatically imply a specific damage increase without checking the weapon's other behavior. Exact current shot intervals and weapon-specific attack-speed scaling are unconfirmed.

## Cooldown Reduction Concerns the Next Attack Cycle

Weapon Cooldown Reduction refers to the pause, recovery, or reload-related stage before a weapon can begin another attack cycle. The historical update archive connects it with the older Weapon Reload Speed label. This makes it relevant when a weapon spends noticeable time waiting between groups of attacks. It is not automatically identical to increasing the speed of shots already being fired.

The researched material does not establish a single standard magazine structure or recovery model for every weapon. A weapon with a short active phase and long pause may respond differently from a continuously attacking weapon.
