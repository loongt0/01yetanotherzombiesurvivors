Yet Another Zombie Survivors Sanji the Rabbit is connected to Vile Wasteland, the Potato item, and the Curly Little Potato achievement. Current community references and later player reports place Sanji at a house window near the east or northeast edge of Vile Wasteland and say one Potato is enough. ## Quick Potato Guide: How to Find Sanji

The short, cross-checked route is to start **Vile Wasteland**, obtain **one Potato** from an item chest, and travel toward the east or northeast edge. Look beyond the playable boundary for a house window with rabbit ears, move close to it, and wait several seconds. Sanji should stand up, hearts should appear, and **Curly Little Potato** should unlock.

If Sanji does not appear, confirm the map, make sure Potato is in the current run, stand closer to the window, and wait rather than moving past immediately. The [current wiki achievement entry](https://yetanotherzombie.wiki.gg/wiki/Achievements_%28Survivors%29), a [later Steam player report](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2163330/discussions/0/3816292540669147857/), and a [post-1.0 route](https://grindnstrat.com/yet-another-zombie-survivors-potato-guide/) agree on the map and window route. Whether Power Overwhelming is a mandatory prerequisite remains unconfirmed.
