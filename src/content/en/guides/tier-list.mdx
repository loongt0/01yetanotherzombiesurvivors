Yet Another Zombie Survivors tier list searches do not currently have enough verified version 1.0 testing to support an honest universal ranking. The strongest choice depends on survival, boss pressure, crowd control, damage tags, available items, and teammate synergies. Tank, Huntress, Ghost, Engineer, Medic, Pyro, Mechanic, SWAT, and Ranger should be compared by their actual roles rather than invented S-tier labels.

## Why a Universal Version 1.0 Ranking Is Not Verified

The [official version 1.0 announcement](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) confirms that the roster now contains nine Survivors. It also documents balance changes affecting characters including Tank, Huntress, and Ghost. Rank 5 expands progression with additional synergy nodes, passive improvements, and a team-wide passive. Rankings produced before these changes cannot automatically represent current performance.

The researched sources do not provide controlled damage comparisons across all nine characters, maps, difficulties, and upgrade combinations. A player's strongest Endless run is a useful case study, not proof that the same lineup dominates Boss Rush or early progression. Therefore, an absolute ordering from first to ninth is unconfirmed. The reliable approach is to compare practical situations and explain what evidence supports each observation.

## Evaluate Survivors by Distinct Performance Categories

New-player survivability asks whether a character can tolerate positioning mistakes and stay useful before a complete build appears. Crowd clearing concerns how comfortably a squad handles groups approaching from several directions. Boss pressure examines sustained or concentrated output against a single important target. Team utility considers whether a character enables another Survivor's abilities instead of acting alone.

Other meaningful categories include control effects, defensive support, damage-tag compatibility, and upgrade accessibility. The official website establishes that items, weapons, abilities, synergies, and permanent upgrades all influence the result. A character can be appealing in one category without being objectively superior everywhere. Exact numerical scores, damage totals, and a weighted overall formula are unconfirmed.

## Tank as a Community-Reported Beginner Option

A [Steam community build discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2163330/discussions/0/599662719724277552/) contains repeated player comments describing Tank as approachable. The researched community wiki identifies Tank as durable and associates the leader bonus with squad armor. It also lists weapon choices including Shotgun, Pump-Action Shotgun, Minigun, and Rocket Launcher. These details support describing Tank as a practical defensive-oriented option.

They do not prove that Tank is the single strongest character or that every player should start there. Minigun and Rocket Launcher use different damage tags, which means their results also depend on item choices and teammate coverage. A Tank-focused squad may combine durability with Medic support or explore offensive interactions with Huntress. The current armor values and comparative win rate are unconfirmed.

## Huntress for Ranged Critical-Hit Pressure

The researched Huntress page describes a ranged Survivor associated with critical hits and boss pressure. Arrow Rain, Bear Trap, and Zombie Decoy appear in the collected character material. The version 1.0 announcement also changes how grouped burst or multi-projectile attacks evaluate critical hits. That makes older critical-damage calculations unreliable without retesting.

Community examples connect Huntress to Tank, Engineer, and Medic through named ability interactions. Bear Trap can connect with Tank's Sawblade Drone, while Engineer's Electrocution can trigger Frost Arrow. Those combinations describe possible build directions rather than guaranteed ranking positions. Exact boss damage, current critical caps, and every upgrade prerequisite remain unconfirmed.

## Ghost, Engineer, and Support-Oriented Comparisons

Ghost is documented as a melee-focused Survivor, while community build discussions pair Ghost with Tank, Engineer, and Medic. The version 1.0 release notes identify changes to Ghost-related abilities, so older footage must be treated as historical. Engineer-related examples include Electrocution, Electric Turret, and interaction with other characters' projectiles. The collected sources do not establish a single best ordering between these characters.

Medic appears in researched synergy examples involving health regeneration and ability interactions. A recorded older endurance build uses Ghost, Engineer, and Medic while discussing slashing damage, weapon cooldown, and survival. That footage shows a documented experiment, not a version 1.0 benchmark. Current support strength, survivability values, and patch-specific interaction damage are unconfirmed.

## Ranger Changes the Shape of Any Current Tier List

Ranger is confirmed as the ninth Survivor and unlocks at Survival Level 175. The developer describes a crossbow-oriented kit, animal companions, support involving critical hits or Luck, and healing options. Older lists containing fewer characters necessarily omit this part of the current roster. Any ranking that excludes Ranger should be identified as incomplete or tied to an earlier version.

The researched [version 1.0 synergy guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3352985777) includes interactions across the expanded roster. It discusses character pairings and squad triangles rather than establishing a developer-approved power ranking. Ranger's usefulness therefore needs to be considered alongside the chosen team, accessible upgrades, and intended mode. An independently verified placement above or below every other Survivor is unconfirmed.

## Compare Squads Instead of Isolated Character Names

The official game website confirms that a run can expand from one Survivor to a maximum of three through SOS recruitment. That structure means a character's value changes when another squad member opens a matching synergy. Community players discuss combinations such as Tank with Medic, Tank with Ghost, and SWAT with Tank and Huntress. The newer synergy guide also explores Engineer with Ranger and other compatible teammates.

Evaluate whether a proposed trio covers immediate survival, crowd pressure, and the objective you actually selected. Then inspect damage tags and check whether the required abilities appear in the current upgrade tree. Historical community labels like electric, cold, toxin, kinetic, or slashing describe build concepts, not guaranteed performance tiers. The strongest trio for every arena and difficulty is unconfirmed.

## Treat Older Videos as Testing Examples

One collected [older endurance-build video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Jxq8919M79Q) demonstrates Ghost, Engineer, and Medic. Its recorded plan mentions Thousand Cuts, Kunai, electric and bleed ideas, Coffee Cup, Solar Panel, and Nuts and Bolts. Those names can help identify what a historical player tested. Because the recording predates version 1.0, its conclusions cannot be presented as current balance facts.

A useful comparison records the same arena, mode, account progression, available items, and squad composition before changing one variable. Repeat the scenario with the same objective rather than comparing unrelated screenshots or different run lengths. Separate developer-confirmed mechanics from community impressions and older footage. Until equivalent version 1.0 tests exist, final tier labels and exact performance numbers remain unconfirmed.
