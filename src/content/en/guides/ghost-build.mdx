The safest answer for a yet another zombie survivors ghost build is to choose a current 1.0 weapon direction first, then recruit teammates whose unlocked nodes reinforce it. Official notes favor retesting Shuriken and Windcutter because both changed in 1.0, while the older Thousand Cuts and Kunai route is only a documented experiment. No source proves one Ghost setup is best in every mode.

## Use the Version 1.0 Ghost Changes First

The [official full-release notes](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) are the strongest baseline for Ghost. Windcutter projectile travel distance increases slightly. Kunai Dance final damage changes from 250 to 150, while Shuriken final damage changes from 350 to 400 and the single-target Shuriken branch gains another 100 damage.

Those changes do not by themselves prove that Shuriken always wins. Travel distance, target count, available upgrades, and the rest of the squad can change the result. They do prove that an Early Access comparison using the old values cannot be copied unchanged. Any precise ranking between the three routes remains **unconfirmed** without a controlled version 1.0 test.

Rank 5 also matters because every Survivor receives new synergy and passive nodes in 1.0. A Ghost build tested without those nodes is not directly comparable to a completed tree. Check the [skill tree guide](/guides/skill-tree/) before treating a missing synergy as evidence that two characters do not work together.

## Shuriken Route and Team Links

The current [Steam version 1.0 synergy guide](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3352985777) documents several Shuriken-related links. With Engineer, Shurikens can deal additional electric damage. With Ranger, Falcon hitting Marked enemies can spawn Shurikens. These are concrete reasons to test teammates rather than adding a popular character without a role.

The same guide lists Engineer and Ranger with Ghost among its five-synergy trios. It also places Ghost and Mechanic with Engineer or Ranger in that group. A high synergy count means more documented interactions are available, but it does not establish the best damage output. The required ability and skill-tree nodes still need to be unlocked and chosen.

A Shuriken page section should therefore identify the exact teammate link, then tell the player to verify it in the live tree. The collected material does not provide a complete official damage formula, proc rate for every interaction, or controlled boss time. Those values remain **unconfirmed**.

## Windcutter Route

Windcutter receives an official travel-distance improvement in version 1.0. A current community answer describes Ghost as highly capable in solo play and names Windcutter as that player's preferred option. This combination of an official change and a current player preference makes Windcutter a reasonable test route, but not an official best build.

Longer travel may improve coverage, yet the sources do not quantify how that change affects total hits in each arena. The available community post also does not provide matched runs against Shuriken or Thousand Cuts. Use the same mode, arena, progression state, and item restrictions when comparing routes. Until such results exist, claims about a fixed percentage advantage are **unconfirmed**.

Windcutter can also be evaluated through team coverage. If Ghost supplies broad slashing pressure, recruits can add defense, control, healing, or a documented synergy. Choose the missing job rather than assuming every high-synergy trio fits the run's objective.

## Thousand Cuts and Kunai Experiment

A transcripted Early Access video starts Ghost in Endless mode and plans an Engineer and Medic squad. The creator prioritizes Thousand Cuts and Kunai, then seeks electric, bleed, cooldown, and defensive items. Engineer later contributes Electrocution, EMP Grenade, Energy Shield, and related support.

This is valuable because it records a complete build hypothesis: leader, recruits, main damage sources, status effects, item goals, and survival tools. It is not current proof because the video predates version 1.0 and Kunai Dance received a large official final-damage reduction. Use the route as a test checklist, not as a copied S-tier label.

To retest it, preserve the electric-and-bleed concept while checking every current ability name and value in game. Record whether Thousand Cuts or Kunai supplies the meaningful output after the patch. The collected sources do not answer that question, so the present version 1.0 result is **unconfirmed**.

## Choose Ghost Teammates by a Named Interaction

The synergy guide records Ghost links with Engineer, Medic, Mechanic, Ranger, and other Survivors. Engineer can connect electric effects with Ghost's projectiles. Medic has a documented link in which Ghost kills restore health, while Mechanic and Ranger provide other listed interactions. These examples allow a build to explain why a teammate occupies a slot.

Current community discussion also says Ghost can operate alone or fit many teams. That flexibility does not remove the need for a plan. In a solo run, Ghost must cover damage and survival through the available upgrades; in a trio, the other two Survivors should either multiply the chosen route or protect its weakness.

Do not promise that a synergy appears automatically when the relevant node has not been unlocked. Version 1.0 spreads synergies across skill-tree tiers II through V. If a player's result differs from the guide, the first checks are tree progress, selected abilities, current patch, and team composition.

## Items and Upgrade Priority

The collected Ghost video prioritizes weapon cooldown and a coordinated group of electric, bleed, and defensive items. That is evidence for one historical experiment, not an official item list for every current Ghost route. The current sources do not establish exact best-in-slot items for Shuriken or Windcutter.

A defensible priority is structural rather than numerical: complete the selected main damage source, take the required synergy ability, and use later choices to support uptime or survival. Avoid spreading early upgrades among unrelated effects merely because they appear in different old builds. If an item's current wording has not been checked in version 1.0, mark it **unconfirmed**.
