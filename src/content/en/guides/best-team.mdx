Yet Another Zombie Survivors best team choices depend on the current arena, mode, available upgrades, item effects, and interactions between up to three Survivors. Research supports combinations such as Tank with Medic, Tank with Huntress, and Engineer with Huntress, while other community examples explore Ghost, Ranger, and Pyro. No official source establishes one universally strongest trio for every version 1.0 situation.

## Start With the Verified Three-Survivor Squad Limit

The [official game website](https://yazs.awesomegamesstudio.com/) states that every run begins with one Survivor. Answering SOS calls can recruit up to two additional characters, creating a maximum squad of three. Solo and duo play are also supported, so a full team is a choice rather than a claim that every run must recruit immediately. The exact best recruitment timing for each arena is unconfirmed.

Choosing the first Survivor affects which leader benefit, starting weapon, and early upgrades become available. Later recruits can add missing defense, control, damage coverage, or a named cross-character interaction. A build should therefore be described as a coordinated package instead of three disconnected character names. The [Steam store page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2163330/Yet_Another_Zombie_Survivors/) independently emphasizes assembling a squad and combining enhancements.
