This Yet Another Zombie Survivors trophy guide separates PlayStation trophies from Steam's much larger achievement list. A current third-party PS5 roadmap lists 1 platinum, 2 gold, 15 silver, and 28 bronze trophies, while its time, difficulty, and missable ratings are author estimates. Use the PlayStation list for trophy totals and cross-check individual conditions before planning a run.

## Keep Platform Totals Separate

The [Steam achievement page](https://steamcommunity.com/stats/2163330/achievements/) currently displays 229 achievements. That number belongs to Steam and should not be used as a PlayStation trophy total. Platform lists can select different milestones even when the underlying game content is shared.

A player moving between PC, PlayStation, and Xbox should maintain separate checklists. One matching objective does not prove that every achievement has a trophy equivalent. Complete platform equivalence is **unconfirmed** without comparing the full live lists.

## Read the PS5 Roadmap as an Estimate

The current [Knoef trophy guide](https://knoef.info/trophy-guides/ps5/yet-another-zombie-survivors-trophy-guide/) lists 46 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 15 silver, and 28 bronze. It estimates 25 or more hours, rates difficulty at 2.5 out of 10, and reports no missable trophies. Those values are useful planning estimates, not developer guarantees.

Playtime depends on skill, progression, chosen difficulty, and how efficiently objectives overlap. Difficulty ratings are subjective and can change after balance patches. Treat any exact completion time or universal difficulty score as **unconfirmed** for an individual player.

## Begin With Story Progression

The trophy roadmap recommends working through Story missions and using Quick or Arcade play when more experience and money are needed. The official [version 1.0 release notes](https://awesomegamesstudio.com/blog/yazs-full-release/) confirm the Camp, 15 main-story missions, 45 Survivor missions, and 19 repeatable missions. This supports Story as a major progression path.

The mission counts do not mean PlayStation awards one trophy for every mission. Follow the active trophy list and the in-game mission interface together. Any claimed one-to-one mapping is **unconfirmed** unless the trophy description states it.

## Use Training Between Difficult Objectives

The roadmap describes spending earned resources on permanent character weapons, abilities, and badges through Training. The official release notes confirm expanded Survivor skill trees, Rank 5, new synergy nodes, passive improvements, and team-wide passive nodes. This makes account progression relevant when a mission becomes difficult.

No official source provides one mandatory Training order for every player. Improve the part of the run that is actually failing, then test again. Exact “best first upgrade” rankings remain **unconfirmed** unless supported by controlled current-version comparisons.

## Understand Run-Level Choices

The trophy roadmap distinguishes weapons and abilities by the icon shown during a power-up choice. It also notes that items come from yellow chests and join the team's inventory. These are third-party observations that should be checked against the current interface if a patch changes presentation.

Select upgrades that reinforce the squad's existing direction rather than collecting unrelated effects. That is general planning advice, not a trophy requirement. A trophy with a weapon-only, ability-only, or other restriction must follow its exact displayed text.

## Cross-Check Individual Boss Trophies

**I'm The Boss** is a useful cross-platform example. [PlayStationTrophies](https://www.playstationtrophies.org/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/trophy/i-m-the-boss.html) says to defeat the final boss of the Isolated City, and [XboxAchievements](https://www.xboxachievements.com/game/yet-another-zombie-survivors/achievement/i-m-the-boss.html) shows the same condition. Steam also lists the same objective.

That agreement confirms the target but does not add a required Survivor, difficulty, build, or time limit. Do not turn a community recommendation into a hidden condition. Any extra restriction remains **unconfirmed** until the platform text or current game states it.

## Leave Optional Torments Outside Platinum Planning

The official 1.0 announcement adds 28 Torments and explicitly says they are not part of the achievement system. They are optional challenge content rather than requirements for full platform achievement completion. A trophy roadmap should not add all 28 to the platinum checklist without direct trophy evidence.

Players can still pursue Torments after the main list for additional challenge. Their mode requirements and rewards should be tracked separately. Whether any individual PlayStation trophy references a specific Torment is **unconfirmed** until checked in the live trophy list.

## Treat Hidden Secrets as Versioned Routes

Curly Little Potato asks players to find Sanji the Rabbit. Current community evidence places the route in Vile Wasteland with one Potato and a house window toward the east or northeast boundary. The official release notes confirm that the current reward changed from Jade Amulet to Easter Egg.

Older footage uses three Potatoes, showing why a trophy guide must state its game version. The Potato drop rate and a claimed Power Overwhelming prerequisite are **unconfirmed**. Follow the current platform objective rather than forcing an Early Access item count.

## Record Each Trophy Attempt

Before a targeted run, note the trophy name, displayed condition, selected arena, mode, squad, and game version. After the run, capture the completion notification or recheck the console trophy list. This separates a successful route from a recommendation that merely sounded plausible.

When a trophy fails to unlock, preserve the same details before changing the plan. One failure does not prove a hidden difficulty, character, or timing condition. Those explanations remain **unconfirmed** until a repeatable test or current platform description supports them.

## Record Progress for Cleanup

After Story and normal progression, group remaining trophies by map, mode, Survivor, restriction, and menu action. Combine objectives only where the displayed conditions are compatible. This can reduce repeated runs without pretending that one roadmap is fastest for everyone.

Save the exact trophy name, condition, completion state, and date checked. If a trophy fails to unlock, record the game version and run settings before retrying. That evidence is more useful than adding an unsupported requirement to the guide.
